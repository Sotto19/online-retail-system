Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about shopping on ShopEase.

How do I track my order?

After placing your order, you will receive an email with your tracking link. You can also view real-time status under My Orders in your account.

What is the return policy?

We offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy on most products. Items must be unused, in original packaging with all tags intact. Initiate a return from My Orders.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery is available in 600 plus cities within 24 to 48 hours. Delivery timelines are shown on the product page.

Are all products on ShopEase authentic?

Yes. We source products directly from brands and authorised distributors. Every product goes through quality checks before dispatch.

How do I apply a coupon code?

Add items to your cart, then enter your coupon code in the Coupon field on the Cart page or during Checkout before placing the order.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel or modify your order within 1 hour of placing it, provided it has not yet been shipped. Go to My Orders and select the order to cancel.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Credit and Debit Cards, Net Banking, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), EMI, and Cash on Delivery.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach us via our Contact Us page, email at support@shopease.in, or call our toll-free number 1800-123-4567 (Mon to Sat, 9 AM to 8 PM IST).
